Country played no part. People already in `people/` on main (Daphne Caruana Galizia, Ján Kuciak) or on an open branch (Javier Valdez Cárdenas) were skipped. ## Which link

`https://blogs.pravda.com.ua/authors/sheremet/576a5b2558832/`, "Рэкетиры Лукашенко на дорогах", 22 June 2016. A plain request from this machine got a 30-byte page saying "You look like a bot, go away." The page was then retrieved in full with a web-fetch tool. It showed the title, the byline "Павєл Шеремет", the date line 22 June 2016, 12:32, the post's text, and the site's notice that a blog reflects only its author's view. His author page on the same site lists the post under his name.

The Internet Archive's availability service returned a capture from 23 June 2016 at 14:07:23 UTC for the same path over `http`. That capture was fetched without requesting a new one. It retains the title, the same date line and the text. The summary in the entry is a paraphrase. No sentence from the post is copied into the entry; the title is quoted and translated.
