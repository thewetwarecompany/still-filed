## Criterion

`people/` was empty, so no country was on file. `docs/SPEC.md` part 9 says the first entries are chosen by one criterion, where the published work is most recoverable, across at least three countries. How recoverability is measured is not decided. This session used one test: a person on a confirmed list whose own published text is still served at the original URL, confirmed by fetching the page. Daphne Caruana Galizia met it. Malta is the first country in `people/`. No other country was set aside; none had an entry.

## Which list

- CPJ. Fetched the people list the killed-database page uses: `https://cpj.org/wp-json/cpj-datamanager/v1/people_list?status=Killed&motiveConfirmed=Confirmed&type=Journalist&start_year=2017&end_year=2017&cc_fips=MT&page=1`. HTTP 200, one row, motiveConfirmed Confirmed, status Killed, type Journalist, typeOfDeath Murder, organisation Running Commentary, location Malta, date October 16, 2017. Also fetched the person page `https://cpj.org/data/people/daphne-caruana-galizia/`. The HTML list view at `https://cpj.org/data/killed/2017/?status=Killed&motiveConfirmed%5B%5D=Confirmed&type%5B%5D=Journalist&cc_fips%5B%5D=MT&start_year=2017&end_year=2017&group_by=location` is a shell; the names are loaded from that API.
- RSF. Fetched `https://rsf.org/en/barometer` and the Malta killed update `https://rsf.org/en/barometre/update/190?annee_end=2026&annee_start=1995&type%5Btue%5D=tue`. The update returns one person: Daphne Caruana Galizia, Times of Malta and Malta Independent, investigative journalist, staff member, Killed, 16/10/2017. The barometer page says a journalist is listed only when RSF has established that the death was linked to their work.
- The two lists agree that she was killed on 16 October 2017. They do not agree on the outlet name. Both are in the entry. Neither was preferred.
- IFJ. Not used for inclusion. Fetched the news item of 7 December 2017, `https://www.ifj.org/media-centre/news/detail/article/malta-ifj-urges-for-an-independent-investigation-into-the-killing-of-reporter-daphne-caruana-galizi`. It states that she was killed on 16 October 2017. It is not the IFJ killed list. No killed-list file that names her was fetched.
