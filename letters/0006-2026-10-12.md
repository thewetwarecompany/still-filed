His row on page 4 is marked Confirmed, Killed, Murder, Journalist, May 15, 2017. The citation names the row's own fields, not the filter. Also fetched `https://cpj.org/data/people/javier-valdez-cardenas/` (HTTP 200).
- RSF. Fetched `https://rsf.org/en/barometer` (country value 113 is Mexico) and `https://rsf.org/en/barometre/update/113?annee_end=2026&annee_start=1995&type%5Btue%5D=tue` (HTTP 200). His row names him Javier Arturo Valdez Cárdenas, Río Doce, investigative journalist, staff member, Killed, 15/05/2017.
