## Lists

CPJ and RSF both list him as killed on 15 May 2017 in Mexico. They give his name and his outlet differently. This entry records both and does not choose.

- Reporters Without Borders, press-freedom barometer, killed, Mexico. The barometer says journalists are listed only if RSF has established that the death was linked to their journalistic activity: <https://rsf.org/en/barometer>. The Mexico killed rows, fetched 12 October 2026: <https://rsf.org/en/barometre/update/113?annee_end=2026&annee_start=1995&type%5Btue%5D=tue>. Country value 113 is labelled Mexico on the barometer form.

CPJ names him "Javier Valdez Cárdenas"; RSF names him "Javier Arturo Valdez Cárdenas". CPJ names the outlets "Ríodoce" and "La Jornada"; RSF names "Río Doce" only.

CPJ's person page states that he was an investigative reporter and editor, that he was fatally shot on May 15, 2017, near the offices of Riodoce in Culiacán, the weekly he co-founded in 2003, and that he was 50. It states that he was also La Jornada's correspondent in Sinaloa, that he wrote a series of books, and that he received CPJ's International Press Freedom Award in 2011. (<https://cpj.org/data/people/javier-valdez-cardenas/>)

The International Federation of Journalists' 2017 killed list was not checked. See `letters/0006-2026-10-12.md`.

## Work

One column, still served at its original address. Checked 12 October 2026. The reader starts here:

<https://riodoce.mx/2017/05/15/malayerba-el-licenciado/>

The page title is "Malayerba: El licenciado". It is in Spanish. The page shows the date 15 May 2017 at 5:04 am, and its `article:published_time` is `2017-05-15T05:04:32-07:00`. The byline on the page reads "Malayerba", the name of the column, not a person's name. Two Ríodoce pages tie the column to him: the site's column menu lists "Malayerba" under "Javier Valdez", and Ríodoce's own report of his death, published the same day at 12:45 pm, calls him the author of the column "Malayerba" (<https://riodoce.mx/2017/05/15/asesinan-al-periodista-de-riodoce-javier-valdez/>). An existing Internet Archive capture from 12 August 2019, <https://web.archive.org/web/20190812134408/https://riodoce.mx/2017/05/15/malayerba-el-licenciado/>, was checked on 12 October 2026. It retains the title, the 15 May 2017 date and the column text.

The column is a short narrative. A man's uncle, ashamed of his addiction, has him taken by force to a rehabilitation centre. Inside, he is beaten again on the order of a man who seems to be in charge, and he learns that this punishment, called the bola negra, is something he must now inflict on others. He rises by doing it for the boss, called the licenciado, earns drugs and parties, and is given the nickname the demonio. When the uncle visits he is told the man is doing well, but he does not get to see him. The column ends with the man incapacitated by drugs and the licenciado ordering the same punishment for him.
