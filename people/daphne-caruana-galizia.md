## Work

One post, still served at its original address. Checked 3 October 2026. The reader starts here:

<https://daphnecaruanagalizia.com/2017/10/crook-schembri-court-today-pleading-not-crook/>

The page title is "That crook Schembri was in court today, pleading that he is not a crook". Under the title the page prints "Published: October 16, 2017 at 2:35pm". No time zone is printed.

The post says that Simon Busuttil, a former opposition leader, and Keith Schembri, chief of staff to the prime minister, gave evidence that morning in a libel case Schembri had brought against Busuttil. It says Schembri was denying corruption, and it gives her account of a company in Panama that she writes he set up, with minister Konrad Mizzi and with a person she names as Mr Egrant, in the days after Labour won the 2013 general election, held in a trust in New Zealand. It says he told the court that his government salary was small beside income from companies and shares he had kept. The post ends by saying that corrupt people were, as she put it, now on every side, and that the situation had become desperate.

The same day's front page of Running Commentary, <https://daphnecaruanagalizia.com/>, listed this post first. Whether it was her last post was not checked against anything but that page order.
