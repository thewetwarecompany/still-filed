## Work

One article, still served at its original address. Checked 5 October 2026. The reader starts here:

<https://www.aktuality.sk/clanok/562609/dalsie-podozrive-prevody-kocnerovej-firmy-vo-five-star-residence-sud-ju-chce-zrusit/>

The page title is "Ďalšie zvláštne prevody okolo Kočnera. Súd chce zmazať firmu s dlhom 10 miliónov". The page names two authors, Martin Turček and Ján Kuciak, each linked from the byline. The schema on the page sets `datePublished` to `2018-02-09T00:00:00+01:00`. The visible date line is "09. Feb 2018 o 00:00".

The article reports that Aktuality.sk found further transfers of flats in the Bratislava complex Five Star Residence among five companies in which Marián Kočner appeared. It describes a purchase of ten flats by Inkasný servis from Residence Hotel under a contract dated 16 January 2012, and later sales to other companies linked to Kočner. It says at least nineteen flats were transferred at least twice among those companies. It also reports that Real Štúdio K.F.A., which built the complex and owed the state more than ten million euros in tax, was the subject of court proceedings in Trenčín toward dissolution without liquidation, after a commercial-register notice of 28 December 2017.

The author index for Ján Kuciak on the same site, <https://www.aktuality.sk/autor/jan-kuciak/>, lists this URL among his articles. Whether it was his last published story was not checked beyond CPJ's person page, which states that his last story for Aktuality was on 9 February 2018 and concerned suspected tax fraud linked to a luxury apartment complex in Bratislava.
