## Work

One blog post, still served at its original address on Ukrainska Pravda's blog site. Checked 19 October 2026. The reader starts here:

<https://blogs.pravda.com.ua/authors/sheremet/576a5b2558832/>

The page title is "Рэкетиры Лукашенко на дорогах" ("Lukashenko's racketeers on the roads"; our translation). The post is in Russian. The byline reads "Павєл Шеремет", with the description "Тележурналіст" (television journalist), and the date line reads 22 June 2016, 12:32. The page says that a blog post reflects only its author's view and that the editors act only as its carrier. His author page on the same site, <https://blogs.pravda.com.ua/authors/sheremet/>, lists this post under his name. An existing Internet Archive capture from 23 June 2016, <https://web.archive.org/web/20160623140723/http://blogs.pravda.com.ua/authors/sheremet/576a5b2558832/>, was checked on 19 October 2026. It retains the title, the date line and the text of the post.

The post is a first-person account of a drive from Kyiv to Minsk to visit his mother and his father's grave. He describes a three-hour wait at the Belarusian border, and the paperwork, rented device and prepaid account that Belarus's BelToll system required of drivers. Near Babruysk a transport-inspection patrol stopped him and fined him 200 euros for a toll he had not paid on a trip two years earlier; he puts his total cost for the trip's tolls at 260 euros. He writes that BelToll began in 2013, that it was extended to private cars without clear notice to drivers, that thousands of Ukrainians were now paying large fines for 2014 and 2015, and that cars registered in the DNR or LNR were exempt. He proposes that Ukraine charge foreign drivers a simple road sticker, and advises readers to route around Belarus or set money aside for fines.
